Personal Life

Thomas Stanley Holland, is a British actor born on June 1, 1996, in Kingston upon Thames, England. He grew up in a supportive family and developed an interest in performing arts when he was young. He attended Donhead Preparatory School, Wimbledon College, and the BRIT School. Tom is approximately 173 centimetres tall. Outside his professional life, he enjoys sports, fitness, and spending time with his loved ones. He is married to American actress Zendaya, whom he met while working on the Spider-Man movies. Tom is admired by many fans for his friendly personality, dedication, and positive attitude.

Career

Tom Holland began his career as a theatre performer and made his West End debut in Billy Elliot the Musical in 2008. He gained recognition for his role in the film The Impossible in 2012. However, he became internationally famous after playing Spider-Man in Captain America: Civil War in 2016. He later starred in successful movies such as Spider-Man: Homecoming, Spider-Man: Far From Home, and Spider-Man: No Way Home. His energetic performances, athletic skills, and natural acting ability have helped him become one of the most recognizable actors of his generation. Tom has also received awards for his performances, including the BAFTA Rising Star Award in 2017..
